01 / INTERVIEWS Tell your story clearly.

Try a simple structure when describing your experience:

  1. Situation: Set the scene briefly.
  2. Task: Explain what needed to happen.
  3. Action: Describe what you did.
  4. Result: Share what changed or what you learned.

Work, school, volunteering, and personal projects can all give you examples. Use real experiences rather than memorizing a script.

02 / GETTING READY Make your first conversation count.
  • Think about the work you want and what you enjoy doing.
  • Know your availability and when you could start.
  • Consider which locations you can reliably reach.
  • Prepare two questions about the role or next steps.
  • Call ahead before visiting for an interview.

A résumé can help, but you can start by introducing yourself.

03 / EMPLOYERS Start with a clearer role.

Before a hiring conversation, write down the job’s purpose, everyday tasks, schedule, location, and expected pay range.

Separate essential skills from skills someone could learn. Plan consistent, job-related questions and clear next steps for candidates.

Use the hiring planner to organize your preparation.
